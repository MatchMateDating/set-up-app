Download the App

Add MatchMate to your home screen — it works just like a native app.

Open MatchMate

Chrome

  1. Open Chrome on your Android device.
  2. Go to app.matchmate.com and sign in.
  3. Tap the menu (⋮) in the top-right.
  4. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  5. Confirm — MatchMate will appear on your home screen.

Safari

  1. Open Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Go to app.matchmate.com and sign in.
  3. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Chrome

  1. Open Chrome on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Go to app.matchmate.com and sign in.
  3. Tap Share in the address bar (or open the menu, then Share).
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
